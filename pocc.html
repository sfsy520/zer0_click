TRON Mismatch PoC — Option A (txID swap bypass)

After interception we swap txID in the broadcast body to txID_T (what imToken signed),
keeping raw_data_hex = approve hex. If the TRON node trusts the submitted txID field
for signature verification instead of recomputing SHA256(raw_data_hex),
the signature verifies (owner) and the approve executes.
